Showcase Screen Concept: The Escapement Floor (Torsional Pendulum & Resonance Physics)
The Concept: Your items do not float, fall, or spin freely. Every single node in the canvas is connected to a shared, mathematically precise Torsional Pendulum Escapement—the beating heart of a mechanical clock. The background is a deep, dark astronomical observatory dome. Across it runs a single, luminous escape wheel, rendered in precise brass geometry. Its teeth click forward exactly one position every 500 milliseconds: a heartbeat that defines the rhythm of the entire UI.
The Interaction (The Magic): This app runs on a single inescapable rule: nothing may move out of time. Every node hangs from an invisible torsional spring, and every node sways in perfect synchrony with the escapement's tick.
•	You cannot simply drag a node. Instead, pressing and holding one winds up its torsional spring, building angular tension that you feel as a slow, escalating haptic resistance under your thumb.
•	When you release, the spring uncoils and the node sweeps in a perfect, mathematically precise arc determined by the tension stored—never chaotic, always governed.
•	The critical detail: if any node's motion falls out of phase with the master escapement tick, a single sharp, discordant haptic "error" pulse fires, simulating the catastrophic "setting" of a poorly adjusted chronometer. The UI then visually shudders once and self-corrects with ruthless precision.
The Visuals: A palette of absolute astronomical precision: Observatory Black (#0C0D10), Polished Brass (#C9A84C), Blued Steel (#3A4B6B), and Parchment White (#F0EDE4). Fine, procedural "rate lines" pulse across the background at the escapement's frequency—like the paper-tape readout of a watch-timing machine. The nodes themselves look like heavy brass poise scales and staking tools, their shadows sharp and cast as if under a single, harsh inspection lamp.
The Focus Transition (The Rate Test): To focus on an item, you cannot simply tap. You must prove your precision. A ghostly "timing gate" appears: a thin blued-steel line that sweeps across the screen at the exact escapement interval. You must tap the target node at the exact moment the timing gate crosses it—like performing a stellar transit observation. If your tap is within tolerance, a clean, resonant haptic chime confirms lock-on. The escape wheel on the background stops completely, frozen in time. The observatory dome seals shut with a deep mechanical thud. The Focus Panel rises with the gravitas of a precision instrument: styled as a heavy mahogany chronometer box with a beveled glass lid, it displays the tool's rate accuracy specification, compensation method, observatory provenance, and the pristine photograph—all held perfectly still, under glass, out of time.
